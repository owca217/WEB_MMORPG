# Parties, friends and item trading

Implement all three requested social features in web and Android through one Social panel. Existing battles remain individual encounters; a nearby party grants the winner bonus XP. Parties hold up to5 characters, with +10% per additional online member within800 world units in the same location, capped at+40%. Qualifying members must belong to the party both at encounter start and victory. Parties and trade invitations are live sessions; accepted friends and pending friend requests persist in PostgreSQL.

Trade is a nearby, same-location item barter outside battles. Both players accept the invitation, offer top-level unequipped tradable items with quantities, then independently confirm the same offer revision. Every offer change clears both confirmations. The server revalidates proximity, ownership, quantities, bind/tradable/unique constraints and container emptiness, and swaps both offers in one transaction. Cancel on disconnect, battle, excess distance or timeout; a transfer already committing must finish before cleanup. No currency exists in the current game, so this adds no money system.

The panel has Party/Friends/Trade tabs, online players for invitations, nickname lookup for friend requests, presence, leave/kick controls, explicit trade offer review and errors. It is available from world and battle, with trade actions disabled during battle. Typed text must release Phaser captures just as chat does. Bound requests, roster and in-memory state; snapshots are individualized and authenticated, with account/session isolation on the client.

Work: shared contracts; party/friend service+migration; atomic trade service/receipt; socket/XP/lifecycle integration; SocialPanel/controller/client state; focused persistence/concurrency/auth/UI tests, two-account browser proof, signed Android0.1.5, deploy and release verification.
